// how_we_test.log

The same five checks, every provider

This is a real sequence — each stage has to pass before a provider moves to the next.

  1. 01

    Baseline speed test

    We measure download, upload and latency without a VPN, then again on five server locations, at three times of day.

  2. 02

    Leak check

    DNS, IPv6 and WebRTC leak tests run on every connection to confirm nothing slips outside the tunnel.

  3. 03

    Kill switch stress test

    We force-drop the VPN connection mid-transfer and confirm traffic actually stops instead of falling back to the open network.

  4. 04

    Logging policy review

    We read the policy against any independent audit or court records, not just the marketing page.

  5. 05

    Support & refund check

    We open a real support ticket and request a refund inside the money-back window to confirm both actually work.
